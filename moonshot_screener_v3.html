📖 Metric Glossary

Plain-English explanations of every number on the dashboard.

🏆 Score (0–100)

The overall rank for this stock as a moonshot candidate. Computed from 7 factors — revenue growth, gross margin, market cap, short interest, cash runway, analyst rating, and price upside. A score of 70+ = High Conviction. 45–69 = Watch list. Below 45 = speculative only.

💰 Price → Target

Price = current market price (live from Finnhub). Target = the average 12-month price target set by Wall Street analysts. The arrow shows the direction. The Upside % column tells you how far the stock would need to rise to hit the target.

📈 Rev% — Revenue Growth

How fast the company's sales are growing year over year. +100%+ means revenue doubled — rare and exciting. Moonshot picks need at least 25–50% to justify the risk. Negative revenue growth is a red flag unless the company just pivoted.

📊 GM% — Gross Margin

After paying the direct cost to make the product, what % of revenue is left? 70%+ = software-like — very good. 40–70% = decent. Below 30% = hardware/commodity — thin margins make scaling hard. High gross margin = pricing power.

⚠ Short% — Short Interest

The % of shares that professional traders are actively betting will fall. Above 20% = heavy bearish pressure — smart money has concerns. 10–20% = elevated caution. Below 5% = no significant institutional opposition. High short interest is a negative signal here.

🏦 Mkt Cap — Market Capitalisation

Total value of the company in the stock market (share price × total shares). Sweet spot for moonshots: $200M – $8B — big enough to be real, small enough to 10x. Under $200M is micro-cap (risky). Over $20B already needs massive growth to double.

⏱ Cash Runway

How many months can the company operate before running out of cash at its current burn rate? 36+ months = comfortable. 24–36 = watch for fundraising. Under 12 months = dilution or failure risk — the company must raise money soon or cut costs.

⭐ Analyst Rating

Average Wall Street analyst recommendation on a 1–5 scale. 1.0–1.8 = Strong Buy. 1.9–2.2 = Buy. 2.3–2.9 = Moderate Buy/Hold. 3.5+ = Hold or Sell. Remember: analysts are often late and conflicted — use as a sanity check, not gospel.

🎲 Risk Level

LOW — profitable or near-profitable, established revenue, low short interest. Suitable for selling CSPs (wheel strategy). MED — revenue growing but not yet FCF positive. Buy shares; CSPs only if IV is high. HIGH — pre-revenue or speculative. Buy shares only, small position ($1–2K max).

📅 Horizon

The estimated time for the thesis to play out — when the catalysts are most likely to hit. 12–18 mo = near-term catalyst visible. 2–4 yr = needs time for technology or market to mature. Matching your holding patience to the horizon is critical — selling early is the #1 mistake.

📊 Backtest (Research Panel)

Simulates buying the stock at the start of every month over 3 years and holding to today. Win Rate = % of entry points that were profitable. Avg Return = average gain/loss. Max Drawdown = worst peak-to-trough drop. Beat SPY = how often it outperformed the S&P 500.
